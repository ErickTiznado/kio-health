## Design Specificity Verdict

Clinical specificity is real but badly distributed. It lives in drawers and modals (ClinicalContext reveal, series-cancel fork, crisis block with tel:, Por cobrar by patient, 4xx/5xx save fork) and disappears in the center of every module: the agenda grid is a Google Calendar clone with clinical stickers; the session editor is a Notion-style block editor where prose is needed; Finanzas is a four-KPI SaaS template; Ajustes/Clínica is generic admin scaffolding in Kio colors.

Deterministic scan: 17 detector findings across 4 rules, of which the scanning agent flagged all 14 gray-on-color plus side-tab (AppointmentPill.tsx:72) and border-accent-on-rounded (SessionPage.tsx:107) as false positives. The detector found nothing real. Greps did: text-[8/9/10px] x49; shadow-lg/xl/2xl without hover x34; icon buttons without aria-label x23; dark:bg-black x8; bg-gradient x10 (DESIGN.md permits 2); font-black x6; bare rounded x4; toISOString in "hoy" computations at ExpenseModal.tsx:24 and WeeklyCalendarGrid.tsx:93/202.

## Overall Impression

Kio has a written, correct design rule adopted by exactly one module. DESIGN.md's first Do is check error before empty state. Dashboard honors it excellently (WidgetError even writes "Esto no significa que esté vacío", and the hook folds isPaused into isError). WidgetError.tsx is imported zero times in Agenda, Finanzas and Clínica. The biggest opportunity is not designing anything new: it is propagating the patterns this team already wrote and validated. confirmAction exists. The correct Intl currency helper exists (PorCobrarTab.tsx:38). The accessible delete button exists (WizardPatientForm.tsx:330). Each lives in exactly one file.

## What's Working

1. WidgetError and the error-before-empty discipline in Dashboard and Pacientes. PatientsPage.tsx:77-83 defeats TanStack placeholderData so a stale empty cannot masquerade as truth.
2. Confidentiality decisions executed as interaction, not badge: diagnosis behind explicit reveal (NextAppointmentWidget.tsx:110-167), availability ramp rebuilt for 4.5:1 and documented in source, reciprocal clinic-scope disclosure (AccessLogPage.tsx:127-133).
3. Honesty where other products lie: patient search claims name-only and the backend enforces exactly that (zero false promises about encrypted fields anywhere); PlanPage invents no prices and documents why; series-cancel presents the real fork without preselecting the destructive option.

### P0 - Two real note-loss paths, and clinical text in plaintext localStorage

notes.store.ts:124 does queue.slice(successCount): if item 0 fails and 1-2 succeed, the slice keeps the two already-synced and deletes the failed one. Any non-tail failure permanently destroys clinical text - on the last-resort recovery path. Second path: handleFinishSession (SessionPage.tsx:130) navigates without consulting hasUnsavedChanges, unmounting the 1s debounce; the guard exists on the back button but not on the button used to end every session. And the full CreatePsychNoteDto - the body the backend encrypts with AES-256-GCM - is written unencrypted to localStorage and persists until a successful sync.

Fix: accumulate failed[] explicitly; extend the guard to handleFinishSession; move the buffer to memory/sessionStorage with a visible unsynced banner and purge on logout().
Command: /impeccable harden apps/web/src/stores/notes.store.ts apps/web/src/pages/SessionPage.tsx

## Questions to Consider

1. Dashboard hides the diagnosis because someone might walk past the desk, and renders the patient's full name at text-3xl on the same surface. Which of those two facts actually identifies a person to a stranger in a waiting room?
2. The session editor is a block tool with drag-and-drop and 7 block types. What would this screen look like if the answer were one 68ch text column, markdown shortcuts, and nothing else - and who would miss the blocks?
3. The last thing the product says about an hour of therapy is "Finalizar Sesión & Cobro" with a dollar sign. Peak-end says that is what the clinician remembers. What if the close led with what was captured - note saved, PHQ-9 12/27, next appointment - and money were the quiet second half?
4. ClinicFinanceTab is the only Finanzas component that checks errors, totals its columns and labels its icon buttons. WidgetError is exquisite and imported in zero of three modules. What fails in the process such that the correct pattern exists in the repo and none of the six neighboring components adopted it?
