## Priority issues that survived both passes

P0 - confirmAction is not a dialog.
lib/confirm-action.ts:45 uses toast.custom. No role="alertdialog", no focus trap, no Escape, and dismissal by any route other than its two buttons never settles the promise — ensureSaved awaits forever and "Finalizar sesion" becomes a dead button. The consequence sentence renders at text-xs text-gray-500 opacity-70 (~2.9:1) and the confirm button is bg-kanji (3.88:1 with white text, exactly what the Regla del Purpura Legible forbids). One file; it repairs all ten destructive confirmations at once, including the five added this session on the assumption it was a real dialog.

P0 - The pending-notes counter points at a read-only editor.
Backend rule verified: upsertPsychNote (appointments.service.ts:479-491) blocks only when 24h have passed AND a note already exists. It does not block by status. But SessionPage.tsx:15 puts COMPLETED in CLOSED_STATUSES and opens readOnly. The counter counts exactly COMPLETED + no note, so 100% of what it counts opens locked and the number can never go down. Pre-existing (present in the pre-session baseline, absent at HEAD), but made more visible by wiring the dashboard straight to /session/:id. The front is stricter than the backend for no reason.

P0 - A failed risk-flag read renders as "no flags".
PatientContextPanel.tsx:32 destructures only data. On failure, with no emergency contact, the entire red crisis block disappears. The session lane had already reported this as an uncorrected honesty hole; this critique rediscovered it independently and rated it P0.

## Agreed across independent reviewers

- The patient record and Finanzas are islands. From a ficha you cannot schedule, open the session, or see a balance; from checkout, the PENDING debt just created leaves no trail to where it lives. PRODUCT.md principle 3 breaks exactly where the clinician stands.
- No modal outside the session module has a focus trap: three in Finanzas, TagsModal in Pacientes, the Agenda drawer.
- role="tab" without the contract (no aria-controls, no tabpanel, no arrow keys) in the ficha and both Sesion tablists — while PatientsPage implements it fully and correctly. The reference already exists in the repo.
